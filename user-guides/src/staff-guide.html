Step 1

Log in and your page

Log in

  1. On the home page use Institution Login.
  2. Choose your Office. Department staff also choose the Department.
  3. Enter the username and password given by the administrator → Login.

Login details are not printed in this guide. Always Log out on a shared computer.

What you see

  • Counters — total pending, in this class, classes waiting.
  • Waiting for review — one chip per class, e.g. CSE · Year 4 6. Click it to open the class.
  • Live — green dot: updates are on.
Daily

Log in → click each chip under Waiting for review → approve who is clear → reject the rest with a clear reason.

Step 2

Approve or reject

Library, College and Department offices. You can handle one student or many at once.

Approve

  1. Open a class (click its chip).
  2. Tick the students who are clear. The top box ticks all.
  3. Press Approve selected.

Use the search box to find a student by name, roll or register number. Approval can't be undone.

Reject

  1. Tick the students and press Reject selected.
  2. Click a ready reason or type your own.
  3. Press Reject. The student sees the reason at once.

Write a reason the student can act on, e.g. “Return the DBMS book”.

Rule

Each student has 2 attempts per office. Your 2nd rejection of the same student pauses them for 24 hours.

Step 3

Hostel Office: check receipts

Hostellers send their SBI reference number and a fee receipt. Each student is one card.

Approve

  1. Press Verify & Approve. The receipt opens.
  2. Check: reference number matches, name and amount are right, receipt is clear.
  3. Press Approve.

Many at once: tick cards → Approve selected → confirm.

Reject

  1. Press Reject on the card.
  2. Pick a reason (unclear receipt, wrong reference, wrong amount, wrong document).
  3. Press Reject Request.

Attempt 2 of 2 · Final → rejecting starts the 24-hour pause.

No receipt required = 7.5% scheme student. Just check and approve.

Step 4

Check a class and download reports

Students Status

Open a class → Students Status. Every student of that year with your office's status:

Completed approved · Pending waiting for you · Incomplete not sent or rejected

Reports (PDF)

  1. Press Generate Report.
  2. Choose Year Wise, Branch Wise or Year + Branch.
  3. Preview Report → Generate PDF.

Department logins

Same page, but only your department's students. Choose only the Year. Reports: Year Wise or Year + Department.

Live updates

  • New request in your open class → list refreshes itself.
  • New request in another class → alert at top right for 8 seconds; press View.
  • The browser tab shows your pending count.
