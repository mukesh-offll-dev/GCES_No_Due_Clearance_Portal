Step 1

Log in and first-time setup

Log in

  1. Open the portal → Student Login.
  2. Type your 12-digit Register Number.
  3. Password:
    First time → your Register Number.
    After setup → your own password.
  4. Press Login.

First time only: complete your profile

  1. Fill Name, Roll No, Date of Birth, Phone and Email.
  2. Create a New Password and type it again.
  3. Press Save & Continue.

Year, semester, department and Hosteller / Day Scholar are set by your Faculty Advisor.

FieldRule
PhoneExactly 10 digits
EmailAn email you can open — reset codes are sent here
New password8+ characters · not only numbers · not too common · not your Register Number
Remember

After setup, only your new password works. Forgot it? See Step 4.

Step 2

Send your requests

Your dashboard has one card for each office you need.

Library, College, Department

  1. On the office card press Send for Verification.
  2. Press Yes, send.
  3. The card shows Pending. Done!

Hostel Office (Hostellers)

  1. Press Send for Verification → Yes, send.
  2. Enter your last SBI Reference Number.
  3. Attach the fee receipt (photo or PDF).
  4. Press Send for Verification.

7.5% scheme students: no receipt needed — just send.

Tip

Use a clear, full photo of the receipt so the name, amount and reference number can be read.

Step 4

Forgot password

Reset it yourself with a code sent to your email.

  1. On the login card press Forgot Password?.
  2. Type your Register Number → Send Code.
  3. Open your email (check Spam too) and copy the 6-digit code.
  4. Type the code and a new password twice → Reset Password.
  5. Log in with the new password.

Good to know

  • The code works for 10 minutes.
  • 5 wrong tries → ask for a new code.
  • Resend code after 60 seconds.
  • No email saved? Log in and use Add your email on the dashboard, or ask your Faculty Advisor.
  • Change email: More details → Change email.
